# 🎤 Demo Script - Presentation Guide

## Overview

**Duration:** 4-5 minutes  
**Goal:** Show how trust-based ranking improves recommendations

---

## 🎬 Demo Flow

### 🎤 Step 1: Introduction (30 seconds)

**Say:**
> "This is a trust-based product recommendation system. Unlike traditional systems that only use ratings, this system analyzes review quality and ranks products by trust score."

**Show:**
- Point to the app title
- Briefly explain the 4 sections

---

### 🎤 Step 2: Select Product (30 seconds)

**Say:**
> "Let me select a product to analyze. I'll choose one with many reviews for a better demonstration."

**Do:**
- Open dropdown
- Select a product with 100+ reviews (e.g., B000KPIHQ4 with 3,807 reviews)

**Say:**
> "This product has [X] reviews. Let's see how they're ranked."

---

### 🎤 Step 3: Show Trust-Ranked Reviews (1 minute)

**Say:**
> "These reviews are ranked by trust score, not just rating. Notice the trust scores in the table."

**Point out:**
- Trust scores column (0.0 to 1.0)
- Status indicators (🟢 Trusted / 🔴 Low Trust)
- Statistics at the top

**Say:**
> "The system automatically identifies suspicious or low-quality reviews. Watch what happens when I filter them out."

**Do:**
- Move the trust score slider from 0.0 to 0.5
- Show how low-trust reviews disappear

**Say:**
> "Notice how [X] reviews were filtered out. These are potentially fake or low-quality reviews that would normally inflate the product's rating."

**Point to:**
- The warning message showing low-trust count
- The filtered count vs total count

---

### 🎤 Step 4: Show Product Score Comparison (1 minute)

**Say:**
> "Now let's compare the traditional average rating with our trust-weighted score."

**Point to:**
- Average Rating metric
- Trust-Weighted Score metric
- Difference metric

**Say:**
> "The average rating is [X.XX], but the trust-weighted score is [X.XX]. That's a difference of [+/-X.XX] points."

**Explain the difference:**

**If positive (+):**
> "The trust score is higher because high-quality reviews boost this product. This is a genuinely good product."

**If negative (-):**
> "The trust score is lower because low-quality or fake reviews are inflating the average. This product may not be as good as it appears."

**Point to:**
- The visual bar chart comparing both scores
- The color-coded explanation message

---

### 🎤 Step 5: Show Ranking Improvement (1 minute)

**Say:**
> "Finally, let's see how trust-based ranking differs from traditional rating-based ranking."

**Scroll to Section 4**

**Point to:**
- Top 10 by Trust Score (left)
- Top 10 by Average Rating (right)

**Say:**
> "Notice that only [X] products appear in both lists. This shows how trust-based ranking identifies different products as top recommendations."

**Point to the comparison stats:**
> "Products only in trust-based top 10: [X]  
> Products only in rating-based top 10: [X]"

**Say:**
> "This demonstrates that our system provides more reliable recommendations by filtering out products with inflated ratings from fake reviews."

---

### 🎤 Step 6: Conclusion (30 seconds)

**Say:**
> "To summarize, this system achieves three key goals:"

**Point to footer or recap:**
1. "Reviews are ranked by trust, not just rating"
2. "Product scores are adjusted for review quality"
3. "Low-quality reviews are automatically filtered out"

**Final statement:**
> "This results in more reliable recommendations and helps users make better purchasing decisions."

---

## 🎯 Key Points to Emphasize

### 1. Trust Score vs Rating
- Trust score considers review quality
- Not all 5-star reviews are trustworthy
- System identifies suspicious patterns

### 2. Automatic Filtering
- Low-trust reviews flagged automatically
- Users can adjust threshold with slider
- Transparent about what's filtered

### 3. Ranking Differences
- Trust-based ranking differs from rating-based
- Identifies genuinely good products
- Filters out products with fake reviews

### 4. Real-World Impact
- Better purchasing decisions
- Reduced fraud
- Improved user trust

## 💡 Tips for Effective Demo

### Before Demo:
- [ ] Test the app beforehand
- [ ] Choose a good product (100+ reviews)
- [ ] Know the trust score difference
- [ ] Practice the flow

### During Demo:
- [ ] Speak clearly and confidently
- [ ] Point to specific elements on screen
- [ ] Pause after each section
- [ ] Engage with audience questions

### Common Questions:

**Q: How is trust score calculated?**
> "It's calculated using machine learning that analyzes multiple factors: review text quality, user behavior patterns, temporal patterns, and rating consistency."

**Q: What makes a review low-trust?**
> "Reviews with suspicious patterns like duplicate content, burst posting, extreme short ratings, or inconsistent user behavior."

**Q: Can users see why a review is flagged?**
> "Yes, the system shows trust scores and status indicators. Users can adjust the threshold based on their risk tolerance."

**Q: How accurate is the system?**
> "The model achieves 93% Spearman correlation and 84% R² score on test data, with 100% precision at top-10 recommendations."
